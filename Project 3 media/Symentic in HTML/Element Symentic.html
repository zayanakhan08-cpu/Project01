Facts about predator animals

Predator animals are hunters that have evolved remarkable adaptations to catch and kill prey. Unlike prey animals with eyes on the sides of their heads for wide vision, predators typically have forward-facing eyes that provide excellent depth perception for judging distances during a chase.
